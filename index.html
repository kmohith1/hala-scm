Inter-Hub Stock Transfer

How Transfers Work

Stock moves instantly from source to destination hub. FIFO lot dates travel with the parts, so ageing stays honest — a 70-day-old controller is still 70 days old after it lands in Guntur. Every movement is logged with the authorising person for accountability.
